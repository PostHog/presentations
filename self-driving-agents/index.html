The case

Self-driving compresses the product loop.

Growth comes from iterating fast. Most of that cycle is spent noticing — finding what broke, why it broke, and what to try next. If the product can do that watching itself and propose the fix, teams get to spend their time on judgment instead. Same team, a much shorter loop.

Section 03

What does self-driving look like?

10

The maturity curve

Four rungs, one direction.

Today
Tracing
Capture every step an agent takes.
Rung 2
Automated monitoring
Evals score those traces, continuously.
Rung 3
Smart monitoring
Cluster failures into reports that explain themselves.
The goal
Self-driving
Reports become signals — and signals become fixes.
11

The last rung

Signals that add up to a fix.

Reports flow into signals. A signal from analytics, paired with one from errors, infra, and the code that ties them together, combines into a single picture: here's what's wrong, here's why, here's the change.

Reports → signals across products → a potential insight ＋ fix.
12

Takeaways

If you remember three things.

  • Self-driving is about speed — compressing the loop between a problem and its fix.
  • It runs on context — analytics, logs, errors, infra, and code in one place.
  • We get there rung by rung: tracing → evals → reports → signals → fixes.
