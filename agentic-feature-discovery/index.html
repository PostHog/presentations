The pipeline

From traces to a report

Traces split into clusters and sentiment, then a scout joins them into a report.

Step 1 · Traces

Messages show your users' wants

You can see intent and sentiment.

User"Can you help me reconcile these two tabs?"
Assistant"Sorry, I can only help with structure, formatting, and formulas."
User"Ugh. That's what I actually needed."
→
Intent help with accounting work
Sentiment negative

Step 2 · Clusters

Similar messages become clusters

Grouped together by embeddings.

Takeaways

What to take home

  • Agents are getting good at discovery, making self-driving possible.
  • Engineers supervise delivery agents; PMs supervise discovery agents.
  • PMs create agentic discovery by building the high-quality context pipelines: evals, signals, scouts.
